Who we are

Growing together in Christ.

Eternal Life Pentecostal Church International was established in 2017 and officially opened in 2018 by Pastor Sebuhoro Daniel.

Our desire is to spread the Gospel, worship God and serve others. We seek to make disciples of Jesus Christ and teach them what Jesus taught.

“To make disciples of Jesus Christ, and to teach them what Jesus taught.”
