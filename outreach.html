A growing charitable initiative

Extending compassion beyond our walls.

Eternal Life Pentecostal Church International intends to expand its charitable outreach through donations to one General Charitable Fund.

The initiative is intended to help refugee families and vulnerable people. Contributions will support the church's ability to develop and deliver practical assistance as plans and resources allow.

We are committed to communicating the purpose of this initiative clearly and responsibly.

Donations are intended to help the church grow its capacity to assist people in need.
