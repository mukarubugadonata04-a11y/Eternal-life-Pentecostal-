Welcome to Eternal Life

Rooted in faith. United in purpose.

Established in 2017 and officially opened in 2018, Eternal Life Pentecostal Church International exists to spread the Gospel, worship God and serve others.

We seek to make disciples of Jesus Christ and teach them what Jesus taught. Whether you are visiting for the first time or looking for a church family, you are welcome here.
